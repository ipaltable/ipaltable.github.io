Restaurant Partner Program

Effective date: June 28, 2026 · PalTable, LLC · Version 2026-06-28

PalTable connects your restaurant with diners who book curated group dining events — shared tables, fixed menus, and a social atmosphere. This page summarizes how the program works at launch. It supplements our Terms of Use.

Who can join

  • Restaurants can sign up in the PalTable iOS app (tap Restaurant on the welcome screen) or preview tools on the web portal.
  • New partners are auto-approved at signup and can publish events right away.
  • PalTable may suspend partners that harm guest trust, violate law, or repeatedly no-show on hosted events.

What to host

  • Dinners only at launch — typically 6:00–9:00 PM seatings.
  • Event-based, not walk-in reservations. You publish a PalTable dinner with a date, time, seat count, menu, and price.
  • Slow nights first. We recommend Tuesday–Thursday evenings. Friday–Saturday prime slots are optional once your events consistently fill.
  • Target 1–2 published events per week while you learn the format.

Pricing & fees

Who paysWhatLaunch rate
Restaurant Monthly platform fee $0 — pay only when seats fill
Pal (guest) Service fee on ticket price 15% added at checkout
Restaurant Receives 100% of the ticket price (via Stripe Connect)

Approval & publishing

  1. Complete restaurant setup in the app (profile, menu, Stripe Connect for paid events).
  2. Create events and tap Publish — they go live to Pals immediately.
  3. We recommend starting with Tuesday–Thursday dinners between 6–9 PM.
